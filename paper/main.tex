\begin{abstract}
Lossy compression of a language model's context memory produces silent
failures: facts the model once held are answered wrongly, with full
confidence, and no signal at generation time. We show that \emph{which}
signals can predict --- and therefore route around --- this loss depends on
the compression regime, and we measure the full picture on one
certification protocol, one frozen 4B model, and a ${\sim}1$B-token
four-domain corpus of 98{,}355 counterfactually filtered probes. Under
\textbf{contiguous eviction}, loss is governed by observable mechanics: a
${\approx}\$140$ dual-encoder gate over text shadows cuts silent loss $72\%$
at a fifth of always-expand cost, ECE $\leq 0.023$. Under a \textbf{trained
compactor}, the same gate class collapses to surface-statistics performance
(AUC $0.55 \approx$ a five-feature logistic at $0.56$): the loss becomes
invisible to write-side and corpus-side signals. It does not become
unpredictable --- it becomes \emph{internal and query-conditioned}. A linear
probe on the frozen model's residual stream (layer 30), with the question
present, predicts answer divergence at $0.819$; a small stack reaches
$0.854$ and, deployed as a gate, matches the shadow gate's correctness at
one third of its expansion budget. A context-only control shows the signal
is \emph{relational}: without the question, no probe recovers it --- linear
or MLP, at any of six layers or over their concatenation (max AUC $0.508$ vs
$0.813$ with the question) --- consistent with sufficiency being a property
of (query, memory) pairs rather than of the memory state alone. We further
show (i) a deployment ladder of increasing cost --- a prompted self-check
($0.638$, one forward, no training), a two-hook attention-head monitor
($0.762$; regime-general --- it reaches $0.783$ under eviction, above the
shadow gate's own discrimination, and transfers zero-shot between regimes),
and the trained probe stack ($0.854$); (ii) that probe
scores rank an instance-independent \emph{fragility ordering} validated
across compactor reseeds ($\kappa{=}0.745$; Kendall $\tau$ $0.57$); and
(iii) a mechanistic surprise: our $1/40$-scale compactor's cross-attention
consists of exact positional delta kernels --- it performs strided
subsampling, not content synthesis (\textbf{position-kernel collapse}; the
learned kernel is a uniform stride-$3$ lattice, identical across heads and
items, and its geometry is loss-blind --- needle-to-kept-position distance
predicts survival at $0.509$), which reframes both its certification failure
and its unexpectedly \emph{better} pointwise retention versus eviction at
matched budget ($0.27$ vs $0.58$ silent loss). Total marginal compute across
all experiments: ${\approx}\$1{,}100$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

``Long context'' conflates a serving problem---fitting the tokens---with two
capability problems that separate the moment full attention becomes
unaffordable: \emph{pointwise} recall of exact, low-redundancy facts, and
\emph{aggregative} understanding of distributed, redundant content. A
needle-class query---an identifier, a figure, a verbatim clause---concentrates
its answer in exact bytes with near-zero redundancy; any lossy transform
destroys it at a rate proportional to the compression ratio. An
integration-class query lives on distributed, redundant information; it is
compression-tolerant and often mildly compression-\emph{benefited}. The two
are not merely different but antagonistic: compression bought for the
aggregative workload is paid for in pointwise fidelity.

The antagonism makes a \emph{router} the critical component: a calibrated
per-query estimate of whether compressed memory suffices. This paper's
question is what information such a router can be built from, and the answer
turns out to be regime-dependent in a way we believe has not been measured
before. Delete tokens, and the damage follows mechanics anyone can see from
bookkeeping features; subsample-and-smear, and the damage moves inside the
model, and further in than that---it is not stored in the memory state at
all, but materializes in the encounter between query and memory, where a
linear probe on the residual stream recovers what every external signal
missed.

Contributions: \textbf{(i)} a two-axis certification protocol (aggregation /
pointwise, never blended) with counterfactually filtered probes;
\textbf{(ii)} its application to two compression regimes at matched retained
budget, showing the axes \emph{trade}---eviction preserves aggregation and
destroys needles; our compactor degrades aggregation while halving silent
needle loss; \textbf{(iii)} the routability result: eviction loss is
mechanics-routable ($0.72$ reduction from shadow features), compactor loss
is routable only by reading the computation ($0.85$ AUC from the residual
stream; $0.56$ from everything outside it); \textbf{(iv)} a three-rung
deployment ladder with measured cost/benefit at each rung; \textbf{(v)}
position-kernel collapse as a failure mode of small-scale learned
compaction, with cross-attention entropy as its certification-time
diagnostic; \textbf{(vi)} instance-independent fragility ordering;
\textbf{(vii)} multi-hop entry-gating beating per-block selection
($1.98\times$ vs $2.33\times$ oracle cost).

\section{Related work}

\paragraph{KV-cache reduction.} Per-context selection scores tokens at
inference and evicts the rest (H2O~\citep{zhang2023h2o},
SnapKV~\citep{li2024snapkv}, StreamingLLM~\citep{xiao2024streamingllm});
amortized selection learns the scorer (KV-Distill~\citep{kvdistill2025});
per-context and amortized synthesis write new compact entries rather than
keeping original rows (Still~\citep{oneill2026still}); architecture-level
lines bake reduction into the model (MLA~\citep{deepseekv2}, native sparse
attention~\citep{yuan2025nsa}). Our work is orthogonal to the choice of
compressor: we certify compressors along two axes and supply the decision
layer their lossiness requires. The strongest published synthesis results
themselves show the axis split we formalize---near-full aggregative utility
with needle-retrieval collapse---which we take as independent evidence for
the two-workload framing.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and abstention.} The gate is a
selective-prediction head~\citep{geifman2017selective} over an external
memory: it estimates, per query, whether the model's conditioning state
supports the answer. The label is \emph{counterfactual} (answers under
compressed vs.\ raw conditioning are compared, rather than correctness being
judged in isolation), and the head is trained fully decoupled from the
backbone. Calibration machinery follows \citet{guo2017calibration} and
\citet{platt1999}.

\paragraph{Probing and representation reading.} Linear probes on
intermediate activations read what a network has computed
\citep{alain2017probes}; recent work reads truthfulness and uncertainty
from residual streams \citep{azaria2023internal}. Our probe is a
\emph{sufficiency} probe over an external memory tier, supervised by
counterfactual behavioral labels rather than truth labels, and---as our
context-only control shows (\S\ref{sec:reads})---it reads a
query-conditioned divergence signal, not a static property of the stored
representation.

\paragraph{Attention sinks and memory-augmented agents.} We repurpose
attention-sink mass~\citep{xiao2024streamingllm}: under-attention to memory
parks probability on sink positions, so sink-mass anomaly becomes an
insufficiency sensor (\S\ref{sec:ladder}). OS-style memory hierarchies
(MemGPT-class~\citep{packer2023memgpt}) page text in and out of context but
do not certify fidelity or route by workload; blended long-context suites
(RULER~\citep{hsieh2024ruler}) are exactly the evaluations our protocol
unbundles.

\section{Two axes and a certification protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Definitions.} Fix a base model $f$, a compression policy $C$, and
a corpus of (context, question, answer) items where each question is
generated from a specific source span. An item is \emph{counterfactually
valid} if $f$ answers it correctly with the context and incorrectly without
it---so a correct answer under compression proves the information traversed
the compressed cache. The \textbf{aggregation axis} is measured over
integrative behavior: next-token KL between $f$ conditioned on the full
cache and $f$ conditioned on $C(\text{cache})$, plus answer-accuracy deltas
on items whose source spans survive compression. The \textbf{pointwise
axis} is the \emph{\lostfact}: among counterfactually valid items whose
source span was compressed away, the fraction answered incorrectly
\emph{without} any expansion mechanism---the silent-failure rate.

\paragraph{Protocol.} A configuration is certified per axis, never as a
blend; the noise floor is established by running the identical probe battery
at zero compression (measured $0.000$); and a configuration certified only
on the aggregation axis may ship only behind an active, calibrated expansion
gate for pointwise traffic. Every report object embeds an
evaluation-protocol record: the exact item partition per axis, the contents
of the compacted context during probing (surviving KV only; no summary
text), confirmation that expansion was disabled, and the scoring rule
(argmax over answer-letter logits, greedy---format failures structurally
impossible).

\section{Regime I --- contiguous eviction: mechanics-routable loss}
\label{sec:eviction}

\subsection{Setup and axis split}
\label{sec:split}
Base model Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (bf16, greedy, pinned runtime
fingerprint). Corpus: 40{,}000 long documents across financial filings
(238M tokens), literature (416M), case law (119M), and source code (154M);
98{,}355 extractive multiple-choice items generated by the base model from
random ${\sim}1$k-token sub-chunks, teacher-verified answerable with context
and discarded if answerable without. Compression policy:
evict-oldest-non-sink at fractions $0.25$ and $0.40$, first four positions
pinned as sinks~\citep{xiao2024streamingllm}; per-item 4096-token prefills
with hash-randomized placement.

The zero-eviction control gives a lost-fact noise floor of exactly $0.000$
(conditioned on prior-correct). Under eviction the aggregation axis barely
moves---mean KL $0.0303$ (\evfrac{0.25}) and $0.0339$
(\evfrac{0.40})---while the pointwise axis collapses: $\mathbf{0.453}$ and
$\mathbf{0.551}$ of facts in evicted spans are silently lost. A
matched-random control (same token count evicted uniformly) inverts the
damage profile---$4\times$ less pointwise loss at $3\times$ the aggregation
KL---so recency-structured policies concentrate pointwise damage exactly
where aggregate metrics cannot see it. One artifact, two workloads, opposite
outcomes.

\subsection{The shadow gate}
\label{sec:shadowgate}
For each item and eviction fraction we produce a counterfactual sufficiency
label ($y{=}1$ iff the compacted-cache answer differs from the raw-span
answer; 196{,}710 rows). The gate is a dual encoder over frozen
Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B with a 256-d normalized projection interaction, a small
feature head (eviction fraction, query--block overlap, evicted flag,
domain), and an auxiliary query-workload head; a frozen-embedder linear
probe sufficed. It is natively calibrated (per-fraction $T{=}1.0$; test ECE
$0.023$/$0.017$); marginal cost ${\approx}\$140$.

Pooled test AUC is $0.829$, but pooling conflates separating evicted from
survived rows (near-deterministic) from the question that matters---among
evicted spans, which lost their fact. Restricting to evicted-span rows, the
full model reaches AUC $\mathbf{0.622}$ ($[0.608, 0.636]$), but the
features-only ablation reaches $0.621 \pm 0.006$---within seed noise of the
full model ($0.624 \pm 0.004$)---while embeddings-only falls to $0.536 \pm
0.004$ (Table~\ref{tab:decomposition}). \textbf{The signal is eviction
mechanics}, and that is enough here. At $\tau{=}0.488$ the gate reaches test
\lostfact{} $0.146$ ($[0.136, 0.156]$) at expansion $0.168$ ($[0.163,
0.173]$), against never-expand $0.453$--$0.551$ and oracle $0$ at $0.194$,
strictly dominating query-type routing at every matched rate
(Table~\ref{tab:gt8}, Figure~\ref{fig:plane}). The head monitor of
\S\ref{sec:ladder} later exceeds this gate's evicted-only discrimination
even in this regime ($0.783$ vs $0.622$, F5); the gate's end-to-end
value---built on calibration and free evicted/survived metadata---stands,
but its discrimination tier is dominated by two attention hooks.

\section{Regime II --- a trained compactor: the axes cross, the gate goes
blind}
\label{sec:compactor}

\subsection{Compactor, certification, no-ship}
Still-canonical per-layer Perceiver (per-KV-head latent banks, $d_l{=}256$,
two pre-norm QK-normed cross-attention blocks, no FFN, ${\approx}50$M
parameters) trained at $1/40$ the published data scale against the frozen
base, certified on the same protocol at matched retained budget (sinks
$+$ $t{=}1024$ latents $+$ 1{,}024 recent raw slots of a 4{,}096-token
context). Aggregation axis: \emph{worse} than eviction (KL $0.263$ vs
$0.122$; recurrence depth 4 collapses to $2.29 \rightarrow$
\texttt{max\_certified\_depth}${}={}0$). Pointwise axis: \emph{better}---
silent needle loss $0.271$ vs eviction's $0.583$ (Table~\ref{tab:compactor}).
The regimes trade axes at this scale; both numbers sit far above the $0.000$
floor, so the gate-mandatory rule re-attaches under every compaction regime
measured.

\subsection{Position-kernel collapse}
\label{sec:collapse}
Captured cross-attention is exactly positional: delta rows, entropy $0.000$
across 1{,}000 items $\times$ 36 layers $\times$ 8 heads. The compactor never
left its position-dominated initialization---compaction here is strided
subsampling of $[K;V]$ plus leakage smear, not content synthesis. Reading
the per-latent argmax source positions directly, the kept set is exactly
$\mathrm{round}(k\cdot(S{-}1)/1023)$, $k{=}0..1023$: a uniform stride-${\approx}2.998$
lattice at source length $S{=}3068$ (density $1/3$), \emph{identical across
all 8 KV-heads and all items} (item-to-item difference $0$). This (a) renames
the regime honestly (subsampling, with true synthesis untested at this
scale); (b) explains the pointwise advantage (subsampled latents smuggle
partial content forward; deletion destroys it); (c) explains the cross-reseed
nesting of failure sets (\S\ref{sec:fragility}); (d) yields a certification
diagnostic: \textbf{report compactor cross-attention entropy; a near-zero
value means the artifact is a subsampler regardless of its loss curve} (ours
hit KL $0.058$ by step 300 while never routing content). We name the failure
mode \emph{position-kernel collapse}, the compactor-side sibling of
recurrent-gate collapse.

\subsection{The gate goes blind}
\label{sec:blind}
A new compaction policy is a new label distribution, so we relabel all
98{,}355 items under the compactor (positive rate drops to $0.167$) and
retrain the shadow gate. The signal decomposition of \S\ref{sec:shadowgate}
\emph{flips}---embeddings-only ($0.567$) now beats features-only
($0.537$)---but everything lands at ${\approx}0.55$, statistically
indistinguishable from a five-feature surface logistic ($0.564$ $[0.553,
0.575]$); engineered interference-geometry features add nothing ($0.568$).
The refreshed gate still \emph{calibrates} (ECE $0.0074$) and rides the lower
base rate to a nominal operating point, but its marginal reduction is
$22.5\%$ at $86\%$ of oracle's budget: it is calibrated always-expand-lite,
not a router. \textbf{The loss did not become smaller-and-harder; it became
internal} (Table~\ref{tab:routability}).

\subsection{The deployment ladder}
\label{sec:ladder}
Three rungs, each with its measured cost: the prompted self-check ($0.638$;
one extra forward, zero training---already above every corpus-side
approach); the head monitor ($0.762$; end-to-end \lostfact{} $0.149$ at
expansion rate $0.060$; two scalar hooks on one forward, no capture
infrastructure---and notably the prompted self-check adds nothing on top of
it ($0.763$ combined): the model's verbalized self-report is subsumed by its
own attention behavior); the trained stack ($0.854$; capture $+$ \$3 of probe
training per capacity point). End-to-end at natural prevalence, the
stack-gated system reaches \lostfact{} $0.146$ at expansion rate
$\mathbf{0.050}$---the shadow gate's correctness at one third of its budget,
strictly inside its curve, with oracle at $0/0.170$. The head-monitor rung
is regime-general (F5): the same eight scalars, with the memory region
redefined as the surviving compacted span, predict eviction-regime loss at
$0.783$ $[0.760, 0.808]$---above the shadow gate's own evicted-only
discrimination ($0.622$)---and transfer zero-shot between regimes
($0.72$--$0.74$ either direction), so under-attention-to-memory is a cheap,
compressor-independent insufficiency sensor. Per-capacity retraining
is required (transfer to $t{=}512$ drops $0.21$, consistent with
cross-capacity label $\kappa$ $0.455$); per-domain temperature repair carries
over from \S\ref{sec:lodo}.

\subsection{What the probe reads}
\label{sec:reads}
A context-only control---the same compact context, no question, same
layer---drops to chance: a fresh probe reaches $0.502$ $[0.487, 0.518]$ and
the question-conditioned probe applied zero-shot reaches $0.508$ $[0.493,
0.523]$, against $0.813$ with the question present. The probe is therefore a
\textbf{behavioral-divergence predictor}---it reads the model's answer-time
state, question included---and should not be interpreted as a readout of
memory contents. We regard this as confirmation rather than deflation: our
certification protocol \emph{defines} sufficiency counterfactually, as a
relation over (query, memory) pairs, and the control shows the model's
internals agree---there is no query-independent sufficiency signal to read,
because sufficiency is not a property the memory state has on its own.
The null holds under a harder test (F4): linear \emph{and} nonlinear
(256-hidden MLP) probes at every layer of a $\{10,15,20,25,30,36\}$ sweep,
and a concat-of-layers probe, all reach at most AUC $0.508$ against $0.813$
with the question --- so there is no query-free sufficiency signal to read
at any probed depth, not merely none linearly decodable at layer 30.

\section{Fragility is a property of the fact, not the checkpoint}
\label{sec:fragility}
Cross-reseed relabeling: $\kappa$ $0.745$ with the weaker instance's failure
set nested inside the stronger's ($P{=}0.945$) and protocol determinism
exactly $1.0$---loss follows an instance-independent fragility ordering that
better compactors peel from the top. Probe scores rank this ordering ($\tau$
$0.57$, CI excludes $0$). Qualitatively, fragility concentrates in
key$\rightarrow$value \emph{binding} under confusable-sibling pressure---
neighboring table rows, near-identical identifiers, common-token values in
crowded neighborhoods---with token rarity mildly \emph{inversely} predictive:
what dies is binding, not content.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{The routability ladder}
One protocol, two regimes, three signal classes:
\begin{quote}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
eviction & loss follows visible mechanics $\rightarrow$ shadow gate $-72\%$; head monitor $0.78$ too \\
subsampling & kernel geometry loss-blind $\rightarrow$ shadows blind ($0.55$), \\
(this work's ``compactor'') & kernel mechanics blind ($0.55$); query-conditioned \\
 & computation-reading $0.85$ $\rightarrow$ same loss at $\tfrac{1}{3}$ budget \\
true synthesis & untested at this scale \\
\end{tabular}
\end{quote}

\subsection{Is subsampling loss mechanics-routable after all?}
No---and the test was as favorable as it could be made. The kernel is fully
known (the exact stride-3 lattice, item-invariant), yet
needle-to-nearest-kept-position distance predicts survival at $0.509$
$[0.499, 0.519]$, the mechanics-with-kernel gate adds $+0.003$ over the
surface floor ($0.550$ $[0.537, 0.563]$), and the residual-stream probe's
scores correlate with kernel distance at $\rho{=}0.045$---almost none of the
$0.85$ is kernel geometry. Because every span of three or more tokens
contains a lattice point, what varies is only the phase of the answer tokens
relative to the lattice, and phase carries no signal: survival is decided by
leakage smear and by how downstream layers use the subsampled cache. The
internality claim stands in full, with one refinement from the context-only
control: the loss is not merely internal to the model---it is internal to the
\emph{query-conditioned} computation. Write-side blind, question-free
read-side blind; only the encounter between the question and the memory
carries the signal.

\subsection{Consequences for memory-system design}
Gate-mandatory is regime-independent; gate-\emph{class} is regime-dependent.
Certification must include the probe: any retrained compactor gets its
divergence-predictability measured per instance and per capacity, its
cross-attention entropy reported, and---if the kernel is nontrivial---the
kernel-geometry test rerun (a full-recipe compactor that escapes
position-kernel collapse may make write-time geometry loss-bearing again;
ours did not). Multi-hop: content-aware \emph{entry} (per-query sufficiency
score) beats per-block ranking ($1.98\times$ vs $2.15$--$2.33\times$
oracle)---the binding constraint was never block scoring.

\section{Limitations}
Single 4B base; one tokenizer (fragility neighborhoods are partly tokenizer
artifacts); the compactor is $1/40$-recipe and mechanistically a
subsampler---Regime II results certify this artifact class, not full-recipe
synthesis. A post-hoc review also found the shipped checkpoint's training
loss used a support-construction variant that double-counted the gold token
whenever it was already in the teacher top-$200$ (corrected in the released
code); all certified numbers measure the checkpoint as trained. Extractive
MCQ probes, letter-match equivalence, greedy decoding, single-turn contexts;
multi-hop $k{=}2$, one evicted candidate per query; the end-to-end
routability point is weighted at natural prevalence on the captured sample;
F1's needle features exclude the $22\%$ of test items without a locatable
in-prefix answer; the context-only null (F2/F4) covers linear and MLP probes
across a six-layer sweep and a concat-of-layers probe (max AUC $0.508$),
leaving depths outside the sweep and probe families beyond MLP untested;
F5's eviction-regime monitor row is measured at $f{=}0.40$ only.
Probe claims are per-capacity and per-instance by measurement
(\S\ref{sec:ladder}, \S\ref{sec:fragility}), and the probe predicts
behavioral divergence rather than certifying memory contents
(\S\ref{sec:reads}).

\section{Conclusion}
Silent memory loss under compression is not one problem. Delete, and the
damage follows mechanics anyone can see; subsample-and-smear, and the damage
moves inside the model---and further in than that: it is not stored in the
memory state at all, but materializes in the encounter between query and
memory, where a linear probe on the residual stream recovers what every
external signal missed, for a third of the routing budget. The practical
statement is a ladder---ask the model (free), watch two attention heads
(cheap), read the residual stream (small)---and the scientific one is that
\emph{routability, not just fidelity, is a certifiable property of a
compression regime.} We think that is the property long-context memory
systems should be certifying next.
