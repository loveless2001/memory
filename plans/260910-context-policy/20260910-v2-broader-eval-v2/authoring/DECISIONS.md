# V2 authoring changes

The inherited `specs.py` and `build.py` reconstruct the same twenty families,
six-slot structure, full original histories and forty synthetic variants.
The initial source-only choices are documented in the preserved v1
`authoring/DECISIONS.md`; their limitations continue to apply.

`revision-intent.json` enumerates the only permitted authored-text edits.
Six answers match the independent reviewer's `suggested_answer` verbatim.
Five other answers and two questions adopt neutral wording. None changes the
named person, requested fact, passage, turn, evidence span or synthetic input.

The same neutral-pronoun rule applies to a named fictional character when gender
is not requested: avoid adding external knowledge unnecessarily. Source-grounded
pronouns in v1 accepted cases remain accepted under their original review; the
style edit does not retrospectively classify them as defects.

No independent acceptance of the edits is claimed. `revision.py` preserves the
original review and separates its unchanged accepts from the twelve cases that
need a delta judgment. Earlier review delegation and central-adjudication limits
are stated in the new report and handoff.
