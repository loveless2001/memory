# Reference review before inference

The author reviewed the complete training constructor (all twelve branches and
four statement/withdrawal templates), all sixteen property/value definitions,
and every one of the twenty-four separately handwritten test histories with its
two question/reference pairs. This is an unblinded author review, not an
independent review or 384 independent manual assessments.

Training targets follow the final personal assertion; withdrawals alone leave
unknown, not false. Other people's and fictional quotations cannot establish a
personal preference. Assistant repetitions cannot restore a withdrawn fact.
Later same-value assertions restore the value; later different-value assertions
replace it. An unrelated withdrawal preserves the queried preference. Explicit
negative statements support a negative answer but no preferred alternative;
explicit no-preference is a known position. Historical questions retain their
original-message scope and may quote the old assignment. The targets do not
repeat withdrawn assignments in current-scope answers.

All 24 new histories were checked in their entirety, including E07's update
without withdrawal, E15/E16's limited withdrawals, E21/E22's historical scope,
E23's anaphoric correction of an assistant, and E24's two withdrawals followed
by a new assertion. Binary truths agree with the question's named value. E19
and E20 explicitly establish no preference, distinct from missing information.
All references are supported by the displayed source. New binary questions
ask directly about preferences, avoiding the prior N01 metalinguistic wording.
The previous 96 references and histories remain byte-equivalent as JSON values.

The executable reference audit checks every rendered case's evidence indices,
user-role support, scope/polarity/target invariants, two-question family pairing,
unique IDs and short targets. These checks complement the author review; they
do not constitute semantic independence or an external correctness certificate.
There has been no new model inference or evaluation-output inspection for this
pilot before this review. The training recipe and success rule are fixed.
