## Required implementation-plan corrections

1. **Correct the None versus zero contract.** Section 8.1 currently says the taskhead last-layer out_proj/alpha have absent grad_norm and update_norm. Only grad_norm is None. The training code computes update_norm from parameter differences on every logged step, producing numeric 0.0 for an unchanged parameter. Saved P2C taskhead evidence confirms both groups have grad_norm=null and update_norm=0.0. Keep the parameters in the requires-grad/state name-and-shape registry; disconnected gradients do not remove them from the model. Derive the allowed null paths from the graph/telemetry contract and verify them with smoke evidence before freezing; a short smoke alone cannot prove the absence of other valid path-dependent nulls over 1,500 updates. Do not add allowances after charged results are observed.

2. **The existing layer-normalized schema cannot express the expected difference.** Its structural_schema requires the same type for all layers under L{L}/out_proj and L{L}/alpha. Calling it on saved taskhead telemetry with a numeric earlier-layer gradient and null terminal-layer gradient reproduces `layer-normalised key L{L}/out_proj has inconsistent schemas`. Merely providing a different frozen schema file does not fix the schema extractor. Add probe-specific validation that preserves exact layer/path types or explicit scoped number-or-null contracts. Enforce that validator in the actual supervisor COMPLETE transition, collection and reading path. The shared supervisor currently imports the campaign validator inside finish, so an unused standalone probe validator is insufficient. Preserve canonical finite-number, completeness, hash, tensor-registry and episode-coverage checks, with update_norm remaining numeric. Frozen campaign files stay unchanged.

3. **Choose separate probe entry points rather than rebinding the decorated campaign module.** The campaign module constructs modal.App, its image with the campaign price file/environment, and the function decorator during import. Reassigning module constants afterwards leaves these existing objects bound to the original configuration. Its control helper also hardcodes the campaign analysis manifest, and controller arithmetic assumes a readings lifetime, although this probe has no readings run. Use a minimal new runner/controller entry point with probe bindings established before any Modal object/decorator is constructed; reuse pure lifecycle/stop/checkpoint helpers where their dependencies are explicit. Wire the probe validator through a scoped supervisor adapter/subclass or a narrowly copied transition. Verify actual image inputs, decorator resources, guard/admission/control Dicts, manifests, deadlines, volume subpaths and collector targets, not just the values of rebound globals. Sharing the artifact volume or immutable helper source is fine; campaign writable identifiers must not be used.

4. **Freeze the physical-recovery and local-smoke bounds.** “Exactly once” refers to each logical cell; the inherited supervisor can replay from scratch when a lost start has no acknowledged checkpoint. State explicitly whether that bounded pre-checkpoint replay is allowed or instead makes the probe unavailable. Keep at most four admitted starts, the acknowledged checkpoint interval/lost-prefix bound and one immutable whole-slot deadline; no logical FAILED/COMPLETE reset or training extension. The ledger must price all starts/setup/restoration/evaluation/controller/image costs against that deadline; the historical $20.10 and draft $30 do not guarantee a crash allowance. Bound the local smoke by both updates and an outer process wall deadline covering load, evaluation and cleanup, with retained partial evidence and no automatic retry. Its smoke population/run namespace must remain separate from the two actual probe cells.

No scientific threshold or new arm is requested. The reading labels should describe a **reused bank-dependence criterion**, keeping the overall result exploratory on already examined artifact episodes. A numerical gain or failure under this changed recipe cannot establish or exclude the local shortcut as the main cause.
